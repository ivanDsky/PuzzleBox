# PuzzleBox 0.32.0 smoothness and glitch baseline (before any polish fix)

Measured 2026-11-15 on the user's own tablet, with the real account. Nothing was changed in the app or the system. Protocol and thresholds come from `smoothness-glitch-report.md` § 5; the static findings S-01..S-23 come from § 2 of the same file.

## 1. Device, build, method

- Device: Samsung Galaxy Tab S9 Ultra, SM-X910, Android 16, panel 1848x2960 used in landscape, 120 Hz active (not touched).
- Build: `com.ivandsky.puzzlebox` 0.32.0, versionCode 1940, release. Dex state: `arm64: [status=speed-profile] [reason=install-spqr]`. Only the release package was measured.
- Link: wireless adb. Taps by `input tap/swipe`, UI read by `uiautomator dump`.
- Cold start: `am force-stop` then `am start -W -n com.ivandsky.puzzlebox/.app.MainActivity`, ten runs, all reported `LaunchState: COLD`.
- Frames: `dumpsys gfxinfo` reset before every flow and read after it. Glitches: `screenrecord` clips of 30 s or less, pulled to `/tmp/polish-baseline/` (not in git), frames extracted with ffmpeg and read by eye.
- Caveats, all weaken the numbers a little:
  - Battery was 31% at the start, 26% at the end (protocol wants 50% or more). Device was on battery, Wi-Fi was slow, and Orca mobile plus a YouTube PiP were running at the start.
  - Recordings are half resolution, 1480x924 at 5 Mbps. Screenrecord itself adds load, so every number is a little pessimistic.
  - gfxinfo counts only frames that were drawn. A still screen is not counted. Framestats keeps at most 120 frames, so slow-frame attribution only covers the tail of a flow.
  - Most flows are one run each. Small samples (Duels list: 17 frames) mean nothing alone.
  - No Perfetto trace was taken. S-01 and S-02 are judged from the cold-start numbers only.
  - The Ranks boards of this account are small (3 to 11 rows), so the 100-row case was not reached.

## 4. Glitches seen on recordings and screenshots

1. Cold start zero state flash (`c2.mp4`, `c2_zoom.jpg`, `c2_zoom2.jpg`). Dark blank launch window for about 180 ms, then "Today" title appears without the rail, and the rail pops in one or two frames later. At video 2.685 to 2.734 s the Home shows zero data for about 75 to 120 ms: "0 days in a row · Best 0", Solved 0, Hint-free 0, Average "–", skeleton circles on the Today card and a skeleton "Play Zip" bar. Real data ("119 days", Solved 500) appears at about 2.76 s and the Recent list at about 2.81 to 2.86 s. Visible, short but clearly wrong numbers.
2. Ranks hero text overlap (`ranks_month.jpg`, `ranks_month2_crop.jpg`). On Global then Month, "3,917 pts" was drawn on top of "Not ranked here yet" in the hero card and stayed so for at least 6 s. It cleared after switching boards. Cause is not proven; `RanksHeroCard.kt` uses `MorphCell`, `RollingLine` and `FrostCell`, so a stuck morph layer is the obvious suspect. This is the only persistent visual defect I saw.
3. Archive hero does not follow the Today chip (`f7_archive.mp4`, `f7_all.jpg`). After tapping archive days, the hero card said "Zip #13 · Sun, Mar 30 2025 · Needs a connection the first time", with a spinner in the LinkedIn chip. After the month arrows and the Today chip the calendar jumped to Nov 2026 (#594 to #608) but the hero card stayed on Zip #13 (also in `duels.jpg`: "Solved on the day"). The selection and the calendar disagree.
4. Replay mixed message (`replay_open.jpg`, `f5_replay_sheet.jpg`). Reached via Ranks, past day Nov 11, "Your solve". The label reads "Your solve · No replay" while the play button is enabled. Board is already drawn when the screen slides in (Zip #604, 40 moves, 0:18).
5. Home Recent selection (`final_home2.jpg`). Tapping a Recent row replaces the Streak panel by a plain "November 14" summary (Zip 0:26, Tango 0:34, Queens 0:47, Strands 7:09, Mini Sudoku 1:11, "You can play this day again from History."). A second tap on the same row did not deselect it. It resets after a fresh launch (`final_home3.jpg`). Possible dead end on tablet; not measured further.
6. "See all" in Recent opens the Ranks tab, not a history list. Odd label/target pair.
7. Tap on a player row in Ranks did nothing (no profile, no feedback).

## 7. Fix first, ranked by measured impact

1. Game load wait (S-21): Zip cold open 3.3 s on a spinner, Strands 1.0 s on a blank screen, Zip warm 0.5 s. Biggest measured number by far. Skeleton or board-first drawing, and find why Zip is slow when cold.
2. Cold start wrong frames (S-03, S-08): 180 ms blank window, rail popping in late, then 75 to 120 ms of zero streak and skeleton data. Hold the first frame until DataStore and Home state are ready, or draw the previous values.
3. Ranks hero overlap (S-09 family): persistent text-on-text on Global/Month. Only visible defect that does not clear on its own.
4. Game-screen jank: janky 11.3% (Queens), 14.4% (Strands), 11.3% (Zip warm), 15.7% (Sudoku run 2), with the 200 ms tick (S-13) as the likely driver. Cheap to fix and easy to remeasure.
5. Tab-switch jank: 4.8% to 5.0% janky, P99 30 to 42 ms, one 53.5 ms frame after 8 s away.
6. Ranks open P99 46 ms and cold partial P99 89 ms.
7. Create tab P50 10 ms, narrow column on tablet.
8. Archive hero not following the Today chip; replay "No replay" label with an enabled play button; "See all" going to Ranks; Recent selection with no way back.
