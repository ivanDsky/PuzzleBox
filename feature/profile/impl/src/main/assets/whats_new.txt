# Player notes for the What's new page, newest first.
# A release adds its entry at the top in the same commit as its version bump.
# "## <version> <date>" starts a release; "- " starts a note; "#" lines are comments.

## 0.41.0 2026-11-22
- A new Train tab teaches the tricks behind Queens, Tango and Mini Sudoku. Pick a technique and it shows you the idea on a small board first.
- Then you practise: find the next move on a board played to the end, step by step, with a short explanation after each move.
- Each chapter ends with a result page, and a finished board gets a short celebration before the next one.

## 0.40.2 2026-11-21
- The home-screen widget keeps showing your last picture while it refreshes, instead of a spinner or empty rings.

## 0.40.1 2026-11-21
- Games, Community and Ranks share Today's large top bar, with your picture at the end on every tab.
- Past you in duels shows your own photo.
- Week and Month boards for a game add up daily points, so several good days beat one lucky one.
- The archive calendar swipes between months, and the arrows slide the same way.
- A game's How to play ends with a "Make your own" link instead of a wide button.
- The Zip editor puts numbers and walls in one mode: tap a cell to number it, tap an edge for a wall.
- In Zip, sliding back over a wrong part of your line cuts it there.

## 0.40.0 2026-11-21
- Monthly recaps: in the first days of a month, Today and Profile open your month as a story of cards, with your solves, fastest game, streak, the moments you earned and a card to share.
- Yearly recaps: from Dec 20, Profile opens your year as a story that ends with all twelve months on one page. Past years stay in Profile.
- A stats page: the stats on a game's page and in Profile open your trends, records and how you compare with friends.

## 0.39.0 2026-11-21
- A home-screen widget shows today's five puzzles with your rank and time, your streak and an Up next button. It fits sizes from one row up to a full page.
- Long-press the app icon for shortcuts to Up next and to each game.
- The Zip and Queens editors and the Solution A | B switch use clear mode tiles.
- Appearance and the Random puzzle source are now lists you pick one row from.
- Ranks Friends boards count friends who finished outside the global top 100.
- Ranks has a Games filter, All games or LinkedIn games, and Settings sets which one Ranks opens on.
- A first solve that isn't your best now shows its rank instead of how far you were from your best.
- The splash mark stays sharp on tablets.
- On a tablet, the game's loading board sits where the board will be.
- Post to LinkedIn waits until LinkedIn says whether it already has your result.
- The loading board shimmers like the rest of the page.

## 0.38.2 2026-11-19
- Ranks Friends boards now count your LinkedIn days, and keep you on days you placed outside the global top 100.
- A friend whose days came partly from LinkedIn now shows as one row on the Week and Month boards.
- Your LinkedIn days keep counting on Ranks while the LinkedIn link is paused.
- The Friends chip on Result, Today and the game page now shows your place on days you finished outside the global top 100.
- Random now opens a day you already solved as a fresh game, not its result.

## 0.38.1 2026-11-19
- Ranks has no Tune icon now: Opens on and Who can see you live in Settings only.
- Haptics now play in dialogs, bottom sheets, popups and dropdown menus too, and Settings, Haptics, Vibration turns them off.
- The light tick when you place a mark now plays on phones whose system dropped it.
- Strands no longer mentions LinkedIn.

## 0.38.0 2026-11-18
- New duel is now its own screen. Adding a puzzle to a duel opens beside it on a tablet, not in a second sheet.
- Filters are now dropdown buttons: tap one to pick from a list, or several at once for Source and Games, in Add puzzle, Community, Random puzzle and Ranks.
- The Hint button is calmer, and hint captions explain the step in a plain sentence before naming any technique.
- Ranks shows when a day's LinkedIn results were last read, and pulling the board down also checks LinkedIn for that day.
- Zip: the line steps back when you back round a corner, turns through a corner you cut and holds at a blocked cell instead of leaning into the one beside it.
- Zip: the next number's old ring is gone from previews, lessons, replays and the editor, and the editor shows one path and one tint when a puzzle has more than one solution.
- The archive calendar keeps the month whole while the next one loads.
- A duel result you have seen on one device now shows as seen on your others, and a puzzle you played on another device counts in the duel.
- On a phone, the first card in Games keeps a gap under the tab strip.
- A Past you rematch that fails no longer talks about an invite.
- Vibration now plays on Samsung One UI phones, where the system had dropped it.
- You can watch your own replay of a duel puzzle you played on your other device.

## 0.37.0 2026-11-18
- There is a new What's new page: open it from Settings, under About.
- Every game now ticks when you place, buzzes when a move is refused and confirms a solve, lessons included. Settings, Haptics, Vibration still turns it all off.
- The first hint you use tells you once that hints are counted on your result.
- A friend's or custom puzzle opens How to play first when you have not seen that game's lesson.
- How to play opens a short rules sheet for the game, with replaying the lesson as a second choice.
- A Tango, Queens or Mini Sudoku hint can place the step it explains with a Place it button.
- Share on a daily result now carries a strip of your solve and a link that opens that day.
- Daily reminders: switch them on in Settings, pick a time, and get one notification a day that says today's puzzles are open.
- Random: the Random chip on each game's calendar opens one puzzle from past days or from New today, narrowed by difficulty.
- Duels are built puzzle by puzzle: Customize lists each puzzle in order, you can drag to reorder, and the add sheet finds puzzles by past day, name, author, code or link, and its filters open from a filter button beside the search. Past you picks from the puzzles you solved.
- A duel's puzzles show as rows, and a rematch keeps the same games in the same order.
- Difficulty now has its own bars beside the level word on Community, Random, the friend puzzle card, the Studio and the duel lineup.
- Days a streak freeze covers now count as streak days.
- Lists no longer kick a bottom sheet when you fling to their end, and a list stretches at its edge without a delay after a page swipe.
- Zip's solve shine follows the rounded board, and dark text on the lime, cyan and orange discs and cards reads better.
- LinkedIn photos load more reliably, and your signed-in photo shows on every avatar of yours.
- A duel you create while New duel closes still hands over its invite code, and a Create in flight no longer shows its notice on another opponent's pane.
- Result shows Already on LinkedIn when that day's LinkedIn result is known.

## 0.36.2 2026-11-18
- Results show your previous and best times side by side, and a sign-in line under your time when you are signed out.
- The full-set result shows the day's total; the times chart gathers slow solves in a last "N+" bar.

- Zip's next number holds your trace colour with a soft halo, also in replays and compares.
- Zip's line follows your finger from where it ends.
- Strands: a drag turn beside letters you already used now joins the free letter; the solved path is thinner.
- On tablets, the Games list has a gap under the tabs, the Recent day pane lists each game as a row, and Duels and Community picks fade in place.
- Loading placeholders shimmer while data arrives.
- Ranks opens on Global until you have a friend.
- Result says why Sign in did not finish.
