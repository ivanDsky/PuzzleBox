# Player notes for the What's new page, newest first.
# A release adds its entry at the top in the same commit as its version bump.
# "## <version> <date>" starts a release; "- " starts a note; "#" lines are comments.

## 0.37.0 2026-11-18
- There is a new What's new page: open it from Settings, under About.
- Every game now ticks when you place, buzzes when a move is refused and confirms a solve, lessons included. Settings, Haptics, Vibration still turns it all off.
- The first hint you use tells you once that hints are counted on your result.
- A friend's or custom puzzle opens How to play first when you have not seen that game's lesson.
- How to play opens a short rules sheet for the game, with replaying the lesson as a second choice.
- A Tango, Queens or Mini Sudoku hint can place the step it explains with a Place it button.
- Share on a daily result now carries a strip of your solve and a link that opens that day.
- Daily reminders: switch them on in Settings, pick a time, and get one notification a day that says today's puzzles are open.
- Random: the Random chip on each game's calendar opens one puzzle from past days or from New today, narrowed by difficulty.
- Duels are built puzzle by puzzle: Customize lists each puzzle in order, you can drag to reorder, and the add sheet finds puzzles by past day, name, author, code or link, and its filters open from a filter button beside the search. Past you picks from the puzzles you solved.
- A duel's puzzles show as rows, and a rematch keeps the same games in the same order.
- Difficulty now has its own bars beside the level word on Community, Random, the friend puzzle card, the Studio and the duel lineup.
- Days a streak freeze covers now count as streak days.
- Lists no longer kick a bottom sheet when you fling to their end, and a list stretches at its edge without a delay after a page swipe.
- Zip's solve shine follows the rounded board, and dark text on the lime, cyan and orange discs and cards reads better.
- LinkedIn photos load more reliably, and your signed-in photo shows on every avatar of yours.
- A duel you create while New duel closes still hands over its invite code, and a Create in flight no longer shows its notice on another opponent's pane.
- Result shows Already on LinkedIn when that day's LinkedIn result is known.

## 0.36.2 2026-11-18
- Results show your previous and best times side by side, and a sign-in line under your time when you are signed out.
- The full-set result shows the day's total; the times chart gathers slow solves in a last "N+" bar.
- Community puzzle results use the same layout as the daily result.
- Zip's next number holds your trace colour with a soft halo, also in replays and compares.
- Zip's line follows your finger from where it ends.
- Strands: a drag turn beside letters you already used now joins the free letter; the solved path is thinner.
- On tablets, the Games list has a gap under the tabs, the Recent day pane lists each game as a row, and Duels and Community picks fade in place.
- Loading placeholders shimmer while data arrives.
- Ranks opens on Global until you have a friend.
- Result says why Sign in did not finish.

## 0.36.1 2026-11-18
